Due to its rarity, lucid dreaming is typically investigated in very small studies. With the ISoLDE project, we aim to create the largest EEG database on lucid dreaming so far. This database will be open for many research groups for different analyses. In addition to the EEG data, we are very interested in best practices of experienced lucid dreamers how to induce lucid dreams.
