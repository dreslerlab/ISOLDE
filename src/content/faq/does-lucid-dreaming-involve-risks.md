---
question: Does lucid dreaming involve any risks?
order: 6
---

There is no indication that spontaneous lucid dreaming poses any risk. Certain lucid dreaming induction strategies may pose some obvious risks, e.g. side effects of sleep deprivation, brain stimulation, or pharmaceuticals. Even purely behavioral strategies may also pose some less obvious risks: developing the habit of repeatedly questioning your reality during the day (is this a dream?) may exacerbate existing dissociative tendencies.
